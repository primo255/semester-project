About linkup HealthCare

Our Story

Founded in 2025, HealthCare Connect was born out of a need to bridge the gap between patients and healthcare providers in Uganda. Our platform was developed by a team of medical and technology professionals from Mbarara University of Science and Technology.

Our Mission

To revolutionize healthcare access by providing a seamless digital platform that connects patients with qualified healthcare providers, reducing wait times and improving overall healthcare delivery.

Our Vision

To become East Africa's leading digital healthcare platform, making quality medical care accessible to everyone, everywhere.
